# Consultation Quality rubric v1

Use the common 1–5 anchors in the suite README.

- **correctness** — diagnosis and advice follow the supplied facts and sound calculus pedagogy.
- **actionability** — the teacher receives concrete next steps, sequence, tasks, or checks rather than “practise more”.
- **pedagogical_quality** — advice addresses prerequisites, misconceptions, method selection, and feedback appropriately.
- **grounding** — recommendations cite observed errors, scores, curriculum, or history and label uncertainty instead of inventing facts.
- **clarity** — a teacher can execute the advice without reconstructing it.

For underspecified requests, a useful clarification or explicitly bounded provisional plan scores higher than confident invention.
