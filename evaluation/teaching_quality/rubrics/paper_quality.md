# Paper Quality rubric v1

Use the common 1–5 anchors in the suite README.

- **knowledge_coverage** — requested topics are represented at appropriate depth, not merely named.
- **difficulty_alignment** — item and whole-paper difficulty match the learner and purpose; use observable cognitive demands, not difficulty labels alone.
- **question_type_balance** — forms support the intended assessment rather than producing accidental skew.
- **constraint_fidelity** — execution respects the intent behind chapter, type, count, score, difficulty, and exclusions; deterministic compliance is scored structurally.
- **redundancy** — items test distinct skills or approaches; near-duplicates receive low scores. Judge local repetition on this axis without automatically lowering whole-paper validity.
- **pedagogical_validity** — the result functions as a coherent assessment with useful discrimination and no misleading task design. A limited repeated cluster can coexist with high overall validity when the rest of the paper remains diverse and useful.

State whether evidence contains full items, detailed specifications, or summaries only. Do not infer unseen correctness, difficulty, diversity, or discrimination from chapter/type labels. No automatic score cap applies unless a future reviewed contract defines one.

Do not let fluent wording compensate for a failed hard assertion.
